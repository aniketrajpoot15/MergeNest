# Learning Log

> **FILE PURPOSE & USAGE GUIDE**
> - **What this file is for:** Your engineering journal. After completing a step or learning something new, record WHAT you learned, WHY it matters, and how confident you feel about it. This becomes your revision sheet before interviews.
> - **Who reads it:** You (for revision) and AI assistants (to know what you've already understood vs what needs re-explaining).
> - **When to update:** After every completed step that teaches a new concept. At minimum, update at every "Learning Update" step (1.10, 2.8, 3.8, etc.).
> - **Format:** One table per week/stage, with columns for Date, Topic, What I Learned, Confidence (1-5), and Key Interview Point.
